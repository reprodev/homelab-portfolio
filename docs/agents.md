## The Intelligence Layer Overview

The **Khurram Nazir Digital Ecosystem** is built using a collaborative "Agentic Trinity" coordinated by a Lead Architect. This document outlines the workflow and prompting strategies used to maintain a premium "Gold Master" standard for the Homelab Site.

#### **3. Codex (Utility Core)**

### **Prompting & Engineering Standards**

- **Premium-First Prompting**: Always specify "Premium Design Language" (e.g., *'Use HSL tailored colors, sleek dark modes, and subtle micro-animations'*).
- **Institutional Aesthetic**: Adhere to the established "Institutional" visual language—minimalist, high-contrast, and authoritative.
- **Verification Loop**: Use browser subagents to verify transitions across multiple viewports and capture "Capture-Safe" screenshots.
